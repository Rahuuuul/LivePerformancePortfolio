Overview

The CSSA Spring Showcase was a large-scale variety performance produced by the UCL Chinese Students and Scholars Association, featuring a wide range of acts including live bands, dance performances, dramatic pieces, and rap.

As Sound Designer and No. 1, I was responsible for the design and operation of the audio system for the event, including microphone deployment, live mixing, line checks, and overseeing the sound department throughout the performance.




Technical Details

Console

- DiGiCo SD9

Show Format

- Variety performance featuring 18 acts

- Included three live bands, five dance performances, three drama acts, and a rap section

Sound Operation

- Responsible for live mixing the full show

- Performed line checks and system checks before each performance

- Created microphone plot and managed input allocation across multiple acts

- Oversaw the sound department during rehearsals and live performance
